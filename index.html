How a candle gets made

  1. 1

    Clay from valley kilns

    Matkas, pots and saucers are bought directly from potter families around the Kathmandu valley, at their price.

  2. 2

    Wax poured at home

    Soy wax is melted, coloured and scented in small batches at artisans’ own homes, around their own days.

  3. 3

    Petals shaped by hand

    Daisies, lotus and peonies are molded petal by petal and set on the wax while it is still soft. No two match.

  4. 4

    Labelled and packed

    Each piece gets the rhododendron label, a wick trim and a box or card backing before it leaves Imadol.
